AArbor

Know how much of a shock your money can take.

Arbor tracks income, spending, debt and savings, then scores your household with the Household Financial Resilience Index (HFRI). Business profiles get a full double-entry set of books.

  • HFRI score from 0 to 100, with the four dimensions behind it
  • Stress tests and seeded Monte Carlo projections
  • Journal, ledger, trial balance and final accounts for businesses
